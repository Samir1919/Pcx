# Product

PCX is a verified used-tech retailer/recommerce platform. MVP prioritizes PCX-owned inventory, Sell-to-PCX acquisition, serial-level inspection, verified listing/passport, checkout, fulfilment, return, and warranty. Open marketplace, AI advisor, advanced PC builder, native mobile apps, and dealer tooling are post-MVP unless reprioritized in an approved spec.
